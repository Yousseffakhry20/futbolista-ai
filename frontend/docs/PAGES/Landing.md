# Landing Page PRD

## Goal

Convert visitors into registered users.

The landing page should clearly communicate:

1. What the platform does.
2. Why it is valuable.
3. Why it is different.
4. What users can achieve.
5. How to get started.

The primary goal is signup conversion.

---

# Target Audience

- Football scouts
- Analysts
- Coaches
- Journalists
- Football fans

---

# Page Structure

## 1. Navigation

Requirements:

- Sticky navigation
- Logo
- Features
- Pricing
- About
- Login
- Sign Up CTA

Desktop:

Horizontal navigation

Mobile:

Hamburger menu

---

## 2. Hero Section

Purpose:

Immediately communicate value.

Content:

Headline:
AI-Powered Football Intelligence

Subheadline:
Analyze players, compare performances, scout talents, and discover football insights using natural language.

Primary CTA:
Start Free

Secondary CTA:
Watch Demo

Visual:

Interactive chat preview or dashboard mockup.

Requirements:

- Strong visual hierarchy
- Responsive layout
- Hero should fit above the fold

---

## 3. Benefits Section

Purpose:

Explain advantages.

Cards:

- Player Analysis
- Team Insights
- Talent Discovery

Requirements:

- Reuse preset card styles
- Hover interactions
- Responsive grid

---

## 4. Example Prompts Section

Purpose:

Show use cases.

Examples:

Compare Vinicius Jr. and Kylian Mbappé.

Who are the best U21 midfielders in the 2025-2026 season?

Analyze Mohamed Salah's last 5 seasons.

Find players similar to Rodri.

Requirements:

- Chat-inspired cards
- Interactive feel

---

## 5. Feature Showcase

Purpose:

Demonstrate capabilities.

Layout:

Alternating content/image sections.

Features:

- AI Chat
- Performance Analytics
- Scouting Reports
- Data Visualizations

---

## 6. Social Proof

Purpose:

Build trust.

Content:

Testimonials or platform statistics.

Requirements:

- Clean presentation
- No fake metrics

---

## 7. Final CTA

Headline:

Start Exploring Football Intelligence Today

Buttons:

- Start Free

---

## 8. Footer

Links:

- Features
- Pricing
- Privacy
- Terms
- Contact

---

# Motion Requirements

Hero:
Fade and slide on load.

Cards:
Subtle hover animations.

Sections:
Reveal on scroll.

Avoid:
Excessive motion.

---

# Mobile Requirements

Navigation collapses.

Single-column layouts.

Touch-friendly buttons.

No horizontal scrolling.

---

# Accessibility

Keyboard navigation.

Semantic HTML.

ARIA support.

Color contrast compliance.

Visible focus states.

---

# Success Criteria

Users understand:

- What the product does
- Why it is useful
- How to start

Within 10 seconds of landing.
